Project Overview

A healthcare facility for AIC Cure International, delivered through all stages from concept design to practical completion and handover in 2018.

As Graduate Interns with Designhaus Studio Architects, we were embedded in the core project team for the duration of the project. This gave us end-to-end exposure to a live, complex building — from the earliest design conversations with the client through to the technical rigour of working and detail drawings, and the practical realities of site execution.

AIC Cure International is a faith-based organisation specialising in orthopaedic and disability care. The facility at Kijabe serves patients from across Kenya and East Africa, and the design had to balance clinical function, patient dignity, and operational efficiency within the constraints of the hillside site.

Working on a healthcare project of this scale and complexity — with a real client, a real programme, and real consequences for the people who would use the building — shaped our approach to every project that followed.
