Project Overview

A contemporary villa set on a spacious plot on the Nairobi–Thika corridor, designed to bring the ease and quality of a mansion to a multi-level residential block format.

The brief called for generous open-plan living and dining on the ground floor, flowing directly onto a landscaped outdoor terrace. Clerestory windows along the upper walls draw deep natural light into the central living spaces without compromising privacy from neighbouring plots.

The upper levels house private bedroom suites, each with en-suite bathrooms and access to generous balconies overlooking the courtyard garden. Bespoke interior detailing — joinery, ironmongery, and finishes — was specified to deliver a considered, coherent interior throughout.

The structural and construction approach was designed for quality and longevity, with careful attention to drainage, weatherproofing, and thermal comfort appropriate to the Kiambu highland climate.
