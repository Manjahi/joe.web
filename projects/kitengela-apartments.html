Project Overview

A multi-unit residential development designed to deliver affordable, modern living to families and working professionals in the fast-growing Kitengela corridor.

The project responds to the rapid urbanisation of the Kitengela area — one of Nairobi's most active peri-urban growth zones — by offering well-designed, compact units that maximise liveable space within a constrained budget.

Each unit was designed with natural cross-ventilation, sufficient daylighting, and durable, low-maintenance finishes appropriate to the local climate. The site layout prioritises shared outdoor space and clear circulation between units, while the structural system was selected for economy of construction and speed of delivery.

The development demonstrates that affordable housing need not mean compromised design — careful planning at the layout stage delivers spaces that feel considered and comfortable at every budget level.
