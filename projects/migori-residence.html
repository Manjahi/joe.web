Project Overview

A private family residence balancing contemporary architectural language with locally-sourced materials — delivering an elegant, comfortable home within a carefully managed budget.

The design was developed in close collaboration with the client family, taking their daily routines, spatial preferences, and long-term plans as the starting point. The brief called for generous communal spaces, well-defined private zones, and a strong connection between indoors and the surrounding landscape.

Responding to the Nyanza climate, the design prioritises natural cross-ventilation through carefully positioned openings, deep roof overhangs for shading, and shaded outdoor living areas that extend the usable floor area without additional construction cost. Locally-sourced stone and timber reduce material costs while grounding the building in its setting.

The result is a home that feels both composed and liveable — one that will serve the family well as it grows and changes over time.
