Our Services

From concept to completion — we handle every stage.

What We Do

We offer a full spectrum of architectural and construction services — designed to take your project from idea to occupied space with minimal friction and maximum quality.

Whether you need a complete design-and-build package or just technical drawings for your own contractor, we structure our engagement around your needs and budget.

Discuss Your Project

Service Areas

Architectural Design & Drafting

Concept development, schematic design, design development, and the production of working drawings and detail drawings to construction standard. We prepare all documentation required for planning submission and contractor tendering.

Construction Management

Full site supervision and construction management, ensuring your project is built to specification, on time, and within budget. We act as your eyes and ears on site so that quality is never compromised.

Planning & Permit Applications

We prepare and submit all planning applications, building plan approvals, and statutory compliance documentation on your behalf, navigating the relevant county and national authorities to keep your project moving.

Interior Space Planning

Thoughtful interior layout and space optimisation — ensuring your rooms work as hard as they look good. From furniture placement to material specification, we bring interior coherence to every project.

Urban Masterplanning

Large-scale neighbourhood and site masterplanning with a focus on sustainability, affordability, and livelihoods integration. Informed by our academic and professional research into the Nairobi housing context.

Architectural Consultancy

Expert advice at any stage of your project — from feasibility and site appraisal to reviewing contractor proposals or resolving on-site design challenges. Consultancy engagements are available hourly or per deliverable.
