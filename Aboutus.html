About Us

Our story, our work, our commitment.

Who We Are

Manjahi ArchArt Design, Construction & Consultancy is a Nairobi-based architectural practice dedicated to delivering bespoke, high-quality built environments for our clients across Kenya.

We believe every Kenyan deserves a home — or space — that is functional, elegant, and achievable within a real-world budget. Our practice was founded on the principle that design excellence and affordability are not opposites, and every project we take on is guided by that belief.

We work closely with each client from the very first brief through to the final handover, maintaining transparency on cost, timeline, and design intent at every stage.

Our Experience

AIC Cure International Hospital

Kijabe, Kenya  |  2018

As Graduate Interns with Designhaus Studio Architects, we were part of the core team across all project stages — from concept design and presentation through to working drawings, detail drawings, and site visits. The project was practically completed and handed over in 2018.

Residential Portfolio

Nairobi & Beyond

Our residential work spans private villas in Ruiru, family homes in Migori, and multi-unit apartment developments in Kitengela — each tailored to the client's brief, site conditions, and budget.

Urban Masterplanning

Nairobi Metropolitan

Our sixth-year masterplan project — a sustainable, affordable mixed-income neighbourhood in the Nairobi Metropolitan Area — addressed the city's housing crisis through cluster-based design, modular unit typologies, and integrated livelihoods planning aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals.
