About Manjahi ArchArt

A Nairobi-based architectural practice specialising in bespoke residential design, construction management, and urban consultancy.

Our work spans individual residences, apartment developments, healthcare facilities, and large-scale urban masterplanning across Kenya. We were part of the team behind the AIC Cure International Hospital, Kijabe — delivered from design through to site handover in 2018 — and have since focused on bringing that same rigour to residential clients who want design excellence within real-world budgets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Start with a free initial consultation — we sit down to understand your brief, site, and budget. We then prepare a design proposal and scope of works for your approval before any fees are committed. No pressure, no obligation.

We are based in Nairobi and have delivered projects across Nairobi, Central Kenya, Rift Valley, and Nyanza regions, including Kitengela, Ruiru, Migori, and Kijabe. We are open to projects nationwide.

For a typical residential project, the design phase takes 4–8 weeks from initial brief to approved drawings, depending on complexity and client feedback turnaround. We keep you involved at every stage.

We offer both full architectural + construction management packages, and design-only engagements where you use your own contractor. We recommend our full-service approach for seamless delivery and quality control on site.
