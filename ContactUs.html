Frequently Asked Questions

Start with a free initial consultation — we sit down to understand your brief, site, and budget. We then prepare a design proposal and scope of works for your approval before any fees are committed. No pressure, no obligation.

We are based in Nairobi and have delivered projects across Nairobi, Central Kenya, Rift Valley, and Nyanza regions. We are open to projects nationwide.

For a typical residential project, the design phase takes 4–8 weeks from initial brief to approved drawings, depending on complexity and client feedback turnaround. We keep you involved at every stage.

We offer both full architectural + construction management packages, and design-only engagements where you use your own contractor. We recommend our full-service approach for seamless delivery and quality control on site.

It helps to bring any title deed or site survey documents, a rough idea of your budget range, and inspiration images or references if you have them. Don't worry if you don't have all of this — we're used to starting from scratch.
